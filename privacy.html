Privacy.

Your enquiry starts a conversation about your project. This notice explains how we handle the details you share.

Who handles your enquiry

MARILEAN SAS is the data controller for project enquiries and talent applications received through the contact links on this website. Our registered office is 60 rue François Ier, 75008 Paris, France. Company registration: SIREN 130 611 445, RCS Paris.

For questions or to exercise your rights, contact contact@epytome.fr, our current contact address.

What we collect and why

When you email us, we receive your email address, message and any project details you choose to include, such as your company or product, intended audience and target release date. We use these details to understand the assignment, respond to your enquiry and arrange a conversation where relevant.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to professional enquiries and developing business relationships. You choose the information to include in your email.

Access and service providers

Access is limited to the people handling your enquiry and the service providers supporting this process. Our contact inbox uses Google Workspace. Google may process technical information needed to operate and secure its services.

Provider processing can take place outside the European Economic Area. Google describes its contractual safeguards, including standard contractual clauses for applicable transfers, in its Cloud Data Processing Addendum. To request further information or a copy of relevant documentation, contact us at the address above.

How long we keep an enquiry

If the enquiry does not lead to a project, we keep it for no longer than three years after the last contact from you. If a project is agreed, the contractual documents are subject to the retention periods applicable to that relationship and to legal obligations.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. We assess each request under the applicable data protection rules. Contact us by email at the address above or by post at our registered office.

You may lodge a complaint with the French supervisory authority, the CNIL.

Browsing and embedded services

The website uses locally hosted fonts and images. Marilean does not add advertising trackers or audience analytics to this version. GitHub hosts the website through its GitHub Pages service and logs the IP address of each visitor for security purposes. GitHub, Inc. is based in the United States and certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. See the GitHub privacy statement.

The Vimeo player loads when you choose to open a film, with its Do Not Track parameter enabled. Essential security cookies may still be used, and your browser may send cookies already stored from earlier visits to Vimeo. See Vimeo's player cookie information.

The contact and creative collaboration pages prepare an email draft in your browser. The page does not store or submit the details you type. You can copy the draft, open your email application, or continue in Gmail. Choosing Gmail passes the draft to Google in a compose link. Marilean receives your enquiry only after you send the email.

Talent applications

The Work with Marilean page helps you prepare a collaboration enquiry by email. We use the professional details you choose to share, such as your role, portfolio, location and availability, to assess possible project collaborations and maintain our talent pool. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in building professional production teams. We keep these details for no longer than three years after the last contact from you. The access restrictions and rights described above also apply to these applications.
