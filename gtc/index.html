SERVICE PROVIDER

These General Terms and Conditions (GTC) apply to any legal entity or natural person (hereinafter "the Client") who has entered into a Quote and/or a Statement of Work (SOW) with the Service Provider.

1. IDENTIFICATION OF THE PARTIES

This agreement is entered into between MARILEAN, whose principal activity is audiovisual strategy consulting and the production of creative works in all formats — primarily video, photography, and 3D modelling — for professionals seeking to enhance their commercial visibility, hereinafter referred to as "the Service Provider", and the Client identified in the Quote, hereinafter referred to as "the Client".

For the purposes hereof, the following terms are defined as follows:

  • "Client": the recipient of the services provided by the Service Provider. Primarily professionals (legal entities), but also, where applicable, individuals.
  • "Service(s)": all audiovisual strategy consulting and creative production services provided to the Client by the Service Provider.
  • "Quote": the initial commercial document issued by the Service Provider, specifying the macro scope of the project and the financial terms. Its signature by the Client constitutes a commercial commitment and acceptance of these GTC.
  • "Statement of Work" (SOW)": a detailed project document concluded after the Quote, specifying in particular the project description, deliverables, scope of services, execution schedule, revision cycles, Client commitments, financial terms, and any specific conditions. Its signature by the Client constitutes full and unconditional acceptance of these GTC.

2. PURPOSE

These General Terms and Conditions govern the audiovisual production, content creation, animation, post-production, consulting, and related services performed by the Service Provider for the Client.

Each project is subject to a Quote, in most cases supplemented by a SOW, the signatures of which give effect to these GTC.

3. APPLICATION OF THE GTC

These GTC are accepted by the Client upon signature of the SOW, or failing that, upon signature of the Quote.

Such acceptance is subject to the legal capacity and authority of the signatory. The signatory must be of legal age, legally capable, or acting under legal representation. Any person signing on behalf of a company is deemed to have the necessary authority to validly bind that company, regardless of their role. By signing the SOW or the Quote, the Client expressly waives their right of withdrawal.

4. NATURE OF SERVICES

The Service Provider will provide the Client with the services described in the SOW. Specific details — deliverables, timelines, and technical specifications — are set out therein.

Services include, without limitation:

  • Audiovisual creative consulting / marketing strategy (needs analysis, creative recommendations, content strategy)
  • Pre-production (project coordination, team management, administrative tasks, casting, location scouting, permits, concept development)
  • Video production (filming, technical management)
  • Photography (photo shoots)
  • Drone operations (aerial photography and filming in accordance with DGAC regulations)
  • Post-production (video editing, colour grading, sound design, photo retouching, export)

The Service Provider reserves the right to make minor adjustments to the services in the event of unforeseen circumstances, in consultation with the Client, while maintaining service quality.

5. DRONE OPERATIONS

For any assignment involving drones, the Service Provider strictly complies with the regulations of the Direction Générale de l'Aviation Civile (DGAC — French Civil Aviation Authority). This includes:

  • Providing at least 5 business days' notice for urban flights to obtain the necessary authorisations;
  • Complying with altitude and distance restrictions based on the environment;
  • Suspending flights in dangerous weather conditions;
  • Respecting image rights when identifiable subjects are visible.

The Client undertakes to provide the information necessary to enable safe and compliant flight planning, and to obtain written consent from landowners for flights over private property prior to any operation.

6. CONTRACTUAL PROCESS AND BOOKINGS

Each project is the subject of an individual SOW drawn up following an assessment of the Client's needs. Services commence upon signature of the SOW, subject to receipt of the deposit provided for in Article 9.

7. AMENDMENTS

MARILEAN's commitment covers only the services explicitly defined in the SOW. Any change in scope must be agreed in writing between the parties and may result in a revision of timelines and/or financial terms.

8. REVISION CYCLES

Each production includes 2 revision cycles to ensure optimal quality while respecting the agreed schedule and process.

  • First cycle: the Client may request all necessary changes, provided they are consistent with the agreements established during the contractual and pre-production phases (script, storyboard, service description, etc.).
  • Second cycle: the Client may only submit requests regarding elements that were revised during the first cycle.

A minimum lead time of 3 business days applies between receipt of the Client's validated feedback and delivery by the Service Provider of the revised version.

9. FINANCIAL TERMS

The financial terms applicable to each project are defined in the SOW. Payment terms (unless otherwise specified in the SOW):

  • A deposit of 50% upon signature of the SOW, non-refundable; Commencement of Services — including any pre-production, scoping, planning, resource booking, or kick-off meeting phase — is strictly conditional upon actual receipt of this deposit. No resources will be mobilised and no execution timeline will begin to run until this deposit has been received in full;
  • Balance of 50% within 30 days of invoice date, the invoice being issued upon delivery of the final deliverables.

Conditional delivery. For projects with a value below €5,000 excl. VAT, the balance is due before access to the final deliverables is provided. The Service Provider shall not be required to deliver the final files or exports until full payment has been received.

Late payment. Any late payment shall automatically give rise to the application of late payment penalties at a rate of three times the applicable statutory interest rate, as well as a flat-rate recovery fee of €40. The Service Provider may also suspend the performance of services in the event of late payment.

Cancellation by the Client. The 50% deposit paid upon signature is definitively retained by the Service Provider. Once the production and/or post-production phase has begun, full payment is due, regardless of the reason for cancellation.

All prices communicated by the Service Provider are expressed exclusive of VAT (excl. VAT), unless otherwise stated.

10. DELIVERY FORMAT

Photos are delivered in .jpeg format, videos in .mov or .mp4 format. Raw footage may be acquired separately under the conditions set out in Article 14.

The Service Provider is bound by a performance obligation with respect to the delivery of the deliverables defined in the SOW within the agreed timelines. However, it is only subject to a best-efforts obligation with respect to the commercial, marketing, or financial performance that such deliverables may generate.

12. CLIENT'S OBLIGATIONS

  • designate a single point of contact;
  • validate the elements submitted within the agreed timelines;
  • actively collaborate in the smooth running of projects.

Liability. The Service Provider shall not be held liable for delays resulting from a lack of cooperation by the Client.

Any postponement exceeding fifteen (15) business days attributable to the Client after validation of the production schedule shall give rise to the invoicing of a flat-rate standby fee equal to ten per cent (10%) of the total SOW amount for each commenced period of thirty (30) calendar days of postponement.
